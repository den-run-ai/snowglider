How to play

Keyboard

← A Turn left
→ D Turn right
↑ W Tuck to speed up
↓ S Brake
Space Jump
V Change camera view

Ski tips

  • Quick turns: Tap left or right for a tighter turn.
  • Carving: Hold a turn for a smooth, wide arc.
  • Braking: Hold down to slow or stop. On steep slopes, brake early.
  • Tucking: Hold up on a clear, straight stretch to gain speed.
  • Hop turns: Jump while steering to turn in the air.

On a phone or tablet
Hold the left or right control to steer, the top-center control to speed up, or the bottom-center control to brake. Tap the center to jump.

Each difficulty has its own personal best. Choose Blue to compete on the global leaderboard.

Press Enter or Space to start from the play button.

Offline play
  1. Load once online. Open SnowGlider and wait until your run is ready.
  2. Come back offline. Use the same browser on this device. Your best times stay here, too.

Sign-in and the global leaderboard need an internet connection.

Add it to your home screen

  • iPhone or iPad: In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Chrome: Choose Install in the browser menu, if available.

Want to join the Blue leaderboard? Sign in above with Google or GitHub.

About SnowGlider

Shred mountains as a snowman on skis, dodging trees and rocks.

  • Find your line

    Follow the checkpoint gates, carve the turns, and chase a faster finish.

  • Pick your pace

    Learn the ropes on Bunny, race on Blue, or take on steeper slopes and jumps on Black and Expert.

  • Stay ahead of the avalanche

    On Blue and above, the snow gives chase. Prefer a calmer ride? Bunny has no avalanche.
